# MDPI Electronics Artifact Claims
This directory maps each major empirical claim in the MDPI Electronics manuscript to its corresponding reproducibility wrapper and validation script:

- claim1_forensic_congestion: Validates RQ1 (workload scaling and in-band telemetry reliability/determinism under T1046).
- claim2_tamper_survivability: Validates RQ2 (in-band deletion survivability under T1610+T1609).
- claim3_structural_visibility: Validates RQ3 (kernel-level eBPF visibility under T1552.007).
- claim4_resource_floor: Validates RQ4 (host resource exhaustion and OOM collector limits).
- claim5_fusion_determinism: Validates multi-source telemetry fusion and cross-run count stability.
